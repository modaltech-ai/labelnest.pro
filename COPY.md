## 5. Modules

**Heading**
One workspace. Twelve places to put things.

**Intro**
Every module links to every other one. A release knows its artists, its tasks, its promo package,
its campaign and its files — so deleting nothing and finding everything is the default.

**Cards**

**Releases**
Catalogue numbers, track listings with ISRC, BPM and key, artwork, credits, press text, UPC and
encrypted revenue splits. Six release types, from single to various-artists compilation.

**Catalogue**
Finished work, separated from work in progress. A cover-art grid you can filter by type and year,
with store links on every card. One button promotes a release into it, and moves it back if you
need to.

**Contacts**
The label address book. 18 contact types from DJ to mastering engineer to playlist curator,
multiple emails and phones, social links, tags, relationships between people, and a merge tool for
when the same person arrives twice. CSV import included.

**Demos & A&R**
A public submission inbox that doesn't touch your personal email. Threaded internal notes, bulk
triage, AI-drafted replies you edit before sending, and one click to turn a demo into a track or a
submitter into a contact.

**Promo**
Build a recipient list from your contacts, send a package, then track each recipient through
Pending, Sent, Delivered, Opened and Downloaded — with their feedback, rating, chart support and
follow-up date recorded against the release.

**Events**
Label nights, festival stages, showcases and livestreams. Lineups with set times, budgets estimated
against actual across nine categories, ticket tiers, guest lists with plus-ones and check-in, and a
wrap-up form for attendance, revenue and what you'd do differently.

**Campaigns**
A content calendar of planned posts with copy, assets and per-item status, plus KPIs you set at the
start and log real numbers against at the end.

**Sample library**
Folders, tags, favourites and waveforms, with a player bar that keeps playing while you browse.
Previews are transcoded in the background, BPM is detected in your browser, and you can zip a folder
or hand someone a share link.

**Tasks**
List or kanban, with priorities, due dates, recurrence, comments and templates that generate a whole
release checklist at once.

**Files**
Everything uploaded anywhere, in one browser, with versions kept and storage usage per module.
Uploads go straight to your bucket, never through us.

**Reports**
Releases, contacts, events, campaigns, promos and activities, summarised over the period you pick.

**Users & audit**
Five roles from owner to read-only, TOTP two-factor on every account, and a searchable log of every
create, edit and delete — who, when, from where, and what the value was before.

## 7. Promo

**Heading**
Find out who actually played it.

**Body**
Promo is where most label admin quietly falls apart. You send sixty packages and get back a mess of
replies you can't summarise for the artist.

Labelnest keeps the list, the send and the response in one record. Build a recipient list from
contacts by type or tag, reuse a list from a previous package, set the embargo, then log what came
back against each name.

**Tracked per recipient**
- Delivery status — Pending, Sent, Delivered, Opened, Downloaded
- Feedback received, and the text of it
- Rating
- Chart support, with chart name and position
- DJ support — confirmed plays, recorded mixes, festival sets
- Follow-up needed, with a date and notes

**Closer**
So when the artist asks how it went, the answer is a record, not a memory.

## 9. Workspace

**Heading**

**Body**
Every label gets `yourlabel.labelnest.pro` the instant the workspace is created. It works
immediately — the wildcard certificate already covers it.

at us. Until a domain is verified, it doesn't resolve at all.

**Note**

flat 404 — they don't reveal whether a workspace exists.

## 10. Own your files

**Heading**
Your masters live in your bucket.

**Body**
Labelnest doesn't resell you storage. You point the workspace at your own Cloudflare R2 account,
and your audio stays on your bill, under your control, in the jurisdiction you choose.

Setup is guided: we check the bucket is reachable, that we can write, that a signed read works, and
that CORS is right — and if any step fails, we tell you exactly what to change, with the JSON to
paste.

**Verbatim quote to feature**
> The platform never becomes a storage reseller.

**Bring your own, throughout**
- **Storage** — your Cloudflare R2 bucket, credentials encrypted at rest
- **Email** — your Resend key and your sender identity, so mail comes from your label
- **Telegram** — your own bot for alerts, with a test button
- **AI** — your own key, and you pick the model

**Leaving**
Export every table as gzipped JSON with an object manifest, written into your own bucket, secrets
redacted. If you cancel, we delete our rows and leave your bucket alone. It's yours.

## 11. Security

**Heading**
Isolation you can point at, not just trust.

**Body**
Labelnest is one deployment serving many labels, so isolation is the whole product. It's enforced
twice: every application query filters by workspace, and PostgreSQL Row-Level Security denies
cross-workspace rows to the database role the app actually runs as — a role that doesn't own the
tables and can't bypass the policy.

**Verbatim quote to feature**
> so a forgotten `WHERE` clause returns nothing instead of another label's data

**Grid**

**Row-Level Security, live**
39 policies, enforced through a restricted runtime role — not a superuser with good intentions.

**Per-workspace encryption keys**
Secrets are encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a key derived per workspace with HKDF-SHA256. A
ciphertext from one workspace can't be decrypted with another's key.

**Two-factor on every account**
TOTP, with recovery codes, encrypted at rest.

**Sessions that expire**
15-minute access tokens. Refresh tokens last 7 days, rotate on every use, and are stored hashed.

**Passwords held properly**
bcrypt at cost 12. Five failed attempts locks the account for fifteen minutes.

**Rate limits per workspace**
100 requests a minute, keyed so one label can't exhaust another's budget.

**A real audit trail**
Every create, edit and delete — with the values before and after, the IP and the user agent. The
application never modifies or deletes an audit entry.

**Nothing vanishes**
Soft deletes on everything other records point at, and a version column that stops two people
silently overwriting each other.

**Support that leaves a trace**
If we ever need to look inside your workspace, it's capped at fifteen minutes and every request is
written into *your* audit log with our name on it.

**Transport**
TLS 1.2 and 1.3 only, HSTS for a year, a strict content security policy, and certificates that
renew themselves.

**Backups**
Weekly `pg_dump`, compressed and shipped off-site to Cloudflare R2, with a guard that refuses to
upload a dump small enough to be corrupt.

**Tested where it counts**
Every change is gated by lint, type-check, migrations and tests against a real PostgreSQL and Redis
— including tests that run as the restricted role and prove one workspace can't read another's data.

**Data handling footnote**
Files are stored in Cloudflare R2's EU jurisdiction by default, or wherever you point your bucket.
The database currently runs in the UK. You are the controller of your data; we process it for you.

## 13. FAQ

**Is Labelnest ready to use?**
The back office is — a working label runs on it every day. The self-serve signup and billing around
it aren't finished, so we're onboarding labels by hand, a few at a time. That's what the waitlist is
for.

**Who is it for?**
Independent labels small enough that everyone knows everyone, and busy enough that nobody can
remember which DJ charted the last EP. It was built for electronic music, but nothing in it is
genre-specific.

**Where does my data live?**
Your files go in a Cloudflare R2 bucket — ideally your own, in the jurisdiction you choose, EU by
default. The database currently runs in the UK. You can export everything, any time, into your own
bucket.

**Can I use my own domain?**
Yes. You get `yourlabel.labelnest.pro` immediately, and you can add your own domain after a TXT
record check. Issuing the certificate for a custom domain is still a step we run for you.

**What happens if I stop paying?**
Your workspace goes read-only for a grace period before it's suspended, and your data stays
exportable. If you cancel, we delete our rows — and leave your storage bucket completely untouched,
because it's yours.

**Do I need a Cloudflare account?**
It's the recommended setup, and it keeps your storage costs on your own bill rather than marked up
on ours.

**What does it cost?**
Not decided yet, and we'd rather say so than invent a number. The shape is a flat monthly price per
label with a seat cap — and every feature available on every plan, with no modules held back for a
higher tier. Waitlist members hear first.

**Is there a mobile app?**
Not yet. It works in a phone browser, but it's built for a desk. An installable PWA is on the list.

**Who's behind it?**
Quest4Goa Records, an independent electronic label, together with the team that builds their
software. We are our own first customer.

## Implementation notes

- **Waitlist endpoint.** `WaitlistForm` POSTs `{ email, label, source }` to
  `VITE_WAITLIST_ENDPOINT`. Point it at Formspree, Buttondown or a Cloudflare Worker — it's a
  one-line change in `.env`. With the variable unset, the form falls back to a `mailto:` handoff and
  says so plainly rather than faking a success state.
- **Contact address.** `VITE_CONTACT_EMAIL` defaults to `hello@labelnest.pro`, which does not exist
  yet. Set it to a real mailbox before launch.
- **Before editing any claim here, read `CLAIMS.md`.** Several plausible-sounding features
  (Spotify integration, calendar view, PDF export, webhooks) are in the product's spec documents but
  not in its code, and must not appear on this page.
